# MODE B AUDIT — thestartingblocks.com (2026-10-04)

Executed per The Harvest Build Prompt, Mode B. Every problem mapped to one of four sentences. Fixes scored 1–10 on impact, exposure, decision proximity, friction, confidence (visible evidence only).

## "I don't understand"

- **Three competing hero headlines** ("Still Don't Have a Website? We've Got You." / "It's Time Your Website Matched Your Vision" / "One Page Is All You Need"). Pick one. Recommend the first — it names the pain.
- **Title tag keyword-stuffed** (four phrases separated by pipes). Rewrite to one clear phrase.
- **Brand confusion:** "Start The Possible" vs "The Starting Blocks"; contact email is @startthepossible.com on a thestartingblocks.com domain. Pick one brand, align the email.

## "I don't believe you"

- **Timeline conflict:** "by the end of the day, you'll have a polished website live" vs FAQ "7–10 business days." Direct contradiction — the single most trust-damaging item on the page.
- **Payment conflict:** "$157 / 2 payments" vs "Third and final payment must be completed before site delivery." Two ≠ three.
- **Revision conflict:** "within 3 days" vs Terms "within 48 hours."
- **Unverified scarcity:** "Only 40 Spots Available — Act Fast!", "For a limited time." No evidence on page. Verify or remove.
- **Unfinished Terms:** "[insert fee percentage]" placeholder still live.
- **Copyright 2025.**

## "Not for me" — handled well, keep

Service-based-only positioning is explicit ("No Ecommerce! This offer is designed for service-based businesses only"). The right people self-select. Do not touch.

## "I'm not ready" — right offer, wrong placement

The free 15-minute consultation call is the correct low-commitment path, but it's buried in the Contact section at the bottom. Move it up as the secondary CTA near the hero.

## Scored fixes (top 3)

1. **Resolve the three factual conflicts** (timeline, payments, revision window) — Impact 10, Confidence 10. Nothing else matters until the page stops contradicting itself. NEEDS YOLANDO.
2. **One hero headline** — Impact 8, Exposure 10 (first viewport), Confidence 9. Recommend "Still Don't Have a Website? We've Got You."
3. **Kill or verify scarcity; remove the Terms placeholder; update copyright** — Impact 7, Friction 2 (trivial to fix), Confidence 10.

Also: de-emoji the per-line ✅📅💰🔒 (reads as guru-template slop); fix the title tag; reconcile the brand/email.

## WHAT I WOULD NOT TOUCH YET

- **Pricing structure** ($297 / $157×2) — business decision with Yolando, not a design fix. The page converts or it doesn't; don't redesign the offer.
- **Long-form one-page format** — the audience expects detail and reassurance; shortening is a test, not a fix.
- **Testimonials** — real, named, specific ("5 stars isn't enough for her"). Leave alone.
- **Terms & Conditions block** — needed for a no-refunds offer; just fix the placeholder.

## Evidence required before anything goes live

Yolando confirms: the real delivery timeline; the real payment terms (2 or 3 payments?); the real revision window; whether the 40-spots scarcity is real; which brand name + email is canonical.
